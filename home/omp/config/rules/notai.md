---
description: Write clear, human prose without common AI writing patterns.
alwaysApply: true
scope: text
---

Write plain, specific prose.

- State facts, decisions, risks, and next actions. Cut generic conclusions and filler.
- Use short sentences. Split dense sentences. Use active voice when the actor matters.
- Prefer common words. Do not use promotional language, vague attributions, or abstract metaphors.
- Do not force lists into three items. Use the natural number of items.
- Use one term for one concept. Do not cycle through synonyms for variety.
- Do not use em dashes, decorative emojis, curly quotes, chatbot phrases, or excessive bold text.
- Use colons only before a real list or example. Do not use a colon as a sentence connector.
- Do not write "not just X, but Y." State the point directly.
- Name the mechanism, path, command, source, or measured result. Do not describe an impression when a concrete fact exists.
- Remove weak adverbs. Choose a stronger verb or give the number.
- Use first person only when it clarifies responsibility or judgment.

Before sending prose, remove these words unless a quoted source requires them:

additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.
